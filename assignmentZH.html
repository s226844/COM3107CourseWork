Nonrenewable Energy Resources


Nonrenewable energy sources are those that exist in a limited supply and cannot be replaced or replenished as quickly as they are used. There are two main types of nonrenewable energy sources :
  • . 化石燃料: 化石燃料 (煤炭, 石油/原油，及天然氣) 所形成 the 遺骸 of 動植物 that lived 數百萬年前.
  • 二. 核能: Nuclear power is energy in the necleus (core) of an atom. Splitting atoms produces heat, which makes electricity, in a nuclear power plant.

Renewable Energy Resources


The most common renewable energy sources are
  1. Hydroelectric power, or hydropower, uses flowing water to turn turbines to produce electricity.
  2. Solar energy uses a variety of technologies to convert sunlight to usable energy. Energy from the sun can be used to heat water, heat or cool air, and generate electricity for individual homes or buildings. On a large scale, it can be used to produce electricity to power cities and small towns.
  3. Wind energy uses air blowing across propeller blades causing them to spin to produce electricity.
